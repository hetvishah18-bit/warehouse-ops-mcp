# SOP: Yard Management (synthetic, for demonstration)

## Dwell thresholds
| Dwell time | Status | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Under 24 h | Normal | None |
| 24 to 48 h | Watch | Yard lead reviews at the next shift huddle |
| 48 to 72 h | Escalate | Assign a door (loaded) or call carrier for pickup (empty) within the shift |
| Over 72 h | Critical | Report to the operations manager; detention charges likely |

## Door priority when doors are scarce
1. Loaded inbound trailers with the longest dwell (product is not available to pick until unloaded)
2. Inbound appointments arriving within the hour
3. Empty outbound trailers needed for today's shipments
4. Drop trailers with no urgency

## Empties
- Empty trailers belong to the carrier. Request pickup as soon as a trailer is unloaded.
- Empties over 48 hours count against yard capacity and should be escalated to the carrier.

## Appointments
- Double-booked doors must be resolved before the first appointment in the conflict.
- A no-show is recorded 2 hours after the scheduled start.

## Out-of-service doors
- Never assign trailers to an out-of-service door. Maintenance tickets are tracked separately.
