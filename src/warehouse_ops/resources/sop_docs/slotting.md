# SOP: Slotting (synthetic, for demonstration)

## Principles
- Slot by velocity: the SKUs with the most pick lines get the closest, easiest locations.
- The golden zone is levels 2 and 3 (waist to shoulder height). Fast movers belong there.
- Level 1 (floor) suits heavy or bulky items. Level 4 (top shelf) suits slow, light items.

## Review cadence
- Run a slotting review monthly, or whenever a SKU's demand changes by more than 50%.
- Use a 30-day velocity window by default; use 14 days during seasonal peaks.

## Executing moves
- Maximum 10 slot swaps per shift, to avoid disrupting picking.
- Do swaps on second shift or weekends, when pick volume is lowest.
- Prioritize swaps by walking distance saved per day.
- Update the location master before physically moving product.

## Replenishment
- Replenish a pick face when it holds under 2 days of supply.
- If there is no reserve stock, check open purchase orders and inbound trailers in the yard.
